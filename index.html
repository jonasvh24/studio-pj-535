The shop

A proper barbershop

Studio PJ is a small, independent barbershop on François Laurentplein where every client gets the full attention of a skilled professional. The team takes pride in precise cuts, careful colour work, and a relaxed atmosphere that makes each visit feel personal and unhurried.

Whether you’re after a classic trim, a bold new colour, or expert styling for curly or wavy hair, the focus is on delivering exactly what you want—no wasted time, just great results.

Services

What we do

Haircuts

Colour services

Custom colour work, from subtle changes to full transformations like bleach and tone.

Styling

Expert finishing for all hair types, with special attention to wavy and curly textures.
